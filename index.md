# Mini Games: Puzzles & Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

[한국어 개인정보처리방침](ko.html)

This Privacy Policy applies to **Mini Games: Puzzles & Arcade** (package name `com.lumenworks.playbox`), provided by LumenWorks (the “App”). It explains how information is handled when you play the included games and use advertising-supported features.

## 1. Information handled by the App

The App does not offer account registration and the developer does not ask for your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or payment information.

The following game data is stored locally on your device:

- remaining plays and whether the initial free plays were granted
- scores, cleared levels, best moves, distance, tower height, and reward gauge progress
- tutorial completion and BGM/sound-effect settings
- rewarded-ad and ad-free benefit state
- counters used to limit how often interstitial ads appear

This local game data is used only to operate the games. It is not sent to a LumenWorks server. If Android system backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up or transfer this app data according to your Google Account and device backup settings.

## 2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share:

- an IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
- app launches, taps, ad views, and other product interactions
- diagnostic and performance information
- advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers where available

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Where required, the App asks for advertising consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can reopen available privacy choices from the App’s Settings & info screen.

Rewarded ads are optional. A reward is granted only after the advertising provider reports that the reward condition was completed. Interstitial ads are limited by a locally stored game-completion counter.

## 3. Firebase services

The App uses these Google Firebase services:

- **Google Analytics for Firebase:** records app opens and game events such as game open, start, finish, and rewarded-ad result. The App does not attach your name, email address, free-form text, or game-save contents to these events.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** receives crash stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata, diagnostic information, and installation identifiers so the developer can diagnose crashes and improve stability.
- **Firebase Remote Config:** receives technical and regional attributes such as country code, language, time zone, platform/OS version, Firebase App ID, package name, and Firebase installation information to deliver game configuration values.

Firebase may process device identifiers and app interaction or diagnostics data as described in Google’s documentation. LumenWorks uses these services for analytics, app functionality, and app stability, not to build a separate profile containing direct identity information.

## 4. Purposes of processing

Information is processed to:

- save and restore game progress on the device
- provide and measure ads and grant optional rewarded benefits
- control game availability, balance values, and initial free-play policies
- understand aggregate game usage
- diagnose crashes, prevent fraud, and improve reliability
- comply with legal and platform requirements

## 5. Data sharing

LumenWorks does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared by Google AdMob and related Google services for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention as described above. Firebase processes information as a service provider for analytics, configuration, and crash reporting. Google’s handling of information is governed by its own terms and privacy policies.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Android backup copies, if any, are controlled through your device and Google Account backup settings.

Analytics, advertising, configuration, and crash data are retained by Google according to the settings chosen by LumenWorks and Google’s applicable retention policies. Because the App has no user account and LumenWorks does not receive a direct identity field with game events, LumenWorks generally cannot identify a particular person’s SDK record from an email request. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID and manage advertising privacy controls in device settings.

## 7. Security

The App limits analytics to predefined game events and does not transmit local save contents to a LumenWorks server. Google SDK network traffic uses encryption in transit. No method of electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children

The App is not specifically designed for children and is not intended for anyone below the minimum age selected for the App in Google Play. LumenWorks does not knowingly request direct personal information from children. Advertising and target-audience settings will follow the age groups declared in Google Play.

## 9. International use

Google may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required by law, advertising consent choices and appropriate safeguards are provided by Google and the App’s consent flow.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, SDK behavior, legal requirements, or store declarations change. The effective date above will be revised when the policy is updated.
